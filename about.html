About the Club

Learn about our history, mission, and the people who make it happen.

📖 Our History

The Tech Innovators Club was founded in 2019 by a small group of students who shared a passion for technology and problem-solving. What started as informal after-school coding sessions quickly grew into a thriving community.


Over the years, we have participated in regional hackathons, robotics competitions, and web design contests. Our members have gone on to pursue careers in software engineering, data science, and cybersecurity.


Today the club has over 40 active members and continues to welcome anyone curious about technology — no prior experience required!

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to inspire and empower students to explore technology through hands-on learning, collaboration, and creative problem-solving.


  • Foster a love for coding and technology
  • Provide a welcoming space for all skill levels
  • Connect students with real-world tech projects
  • Build teamwork and communication skills
  • Prepare members for future careers in STEM
